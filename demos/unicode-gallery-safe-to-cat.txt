========================================================================
secure-terminal Unicode gallery -- safe to cat.
Unicode 15.1.0. A plain terminal shows this flat; secure-terminal tints
every character by risk class and boxes what has no safe glyph.
Grids below emit only graphic glyphs (safe anywhere). The RISK
sections carry raw control/bidi/invisible bytes, one per line, each
control terminated so the terminal returns to ground state before the
newline: bounded, no persistent state, no `reset` needed (a BEL beeps).
========================================================================
